GYRO GOLF WORLD TOUR — NEXT STEPS

1. Unzip Gyro-Golf-World-Tour-Render.zip.
2. Open your gyro-golf-world-tour repository on GitHub.
3. Click Add file, then Upload files.
4. Drag in ALL unzipped items: public, server.js, package.json,
   package-lock.json, render.yaml, README.md, scripts, and .gitignore.
5. Commit the files directly to main.
6. In Render click New +, choose Blueprint, connect this repository,
   and click Deploy Blueprint.
7. Open the Render URL. Create a room and give the six-character code
   to Player 2.
